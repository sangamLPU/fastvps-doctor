# A note to the FastVPS team

Dear FastVPS team,

Thank you for supporting my VPS as a student. I have been learning Linux operations and Python, and I wanted to make a practical contribution in return. I independently built FastVPS Doctor, a small command-line tool that gathers selected guest-system diagnostics and prepares a local report for a customer to review before sharing.

I designed it to complement your public tools and infrastructure monitoring rather than reproduce them. It does not connect to FastVPS systems, collect credentials, upload reports, or claim to assign support responsibility. The current version is an early prototype, and its compatibility and diagnostic coverage still need broader Linux validation.

If you have time, I would appreciate technical feedback on whether the report format, privacy defaults and guest-level checks could be useful to customers or support engineers. I understand that any adoption would require your own review and approval.

With appreciation,

**A student developer and FastVPS customer**
